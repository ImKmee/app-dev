# The Amazing World of Gumball

It is an animated comedy series from *Cartoon Network* that mixes 2D, 3D, live-action, and stop-motion styles in one show. It follows a 12-year-old cat and his family as they get into absurd trouble in the town of Elmore. It contains a lot of pop-culture references, fast-paced humor that I like, and creative dialogue that stays in my mind for a long time. It is one of the cartoons I grew up with in the mid 2010s and still revisit from time to time. The older I got, the better the series became.
